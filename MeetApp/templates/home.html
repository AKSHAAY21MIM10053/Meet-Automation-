Transform doctor-patient interactions with our advanced AI assistant


Simply share the meeting link, and our bot automatically joins, listens attentively, and captures key details. From treatment plans and medication schedules to follow-up appointments, it generates precise summaries in both English and the patient’s native language. Summaries are delivered via email and accessible on our platform for seamless reference.

With intelligent suggestions tailored to the conversation, our AI empowers better understanding, fosters stronger connections, and enhances patient engagement.

Why Choose Our Solution?

  • Automated Efficiency: Seamlessly captures and organizes essential meeting details.
  • Multilingual Support: Provides summaries in English and local languages for better accessibility.
  • Actionable Insights: Delivers smart suggestions based on discussion outcomes.
  • Enhanced Retention: Helps patients stay informed and recall critical information.
  • Convenient Access: Summaries are sent via email and available on the platform anytime.
  • Strengthened Communication: Facilitates clearer and more effective interactions.

Doctor Initiates: The doctor creates an account for the patient on the platform by entering their basic details.
Email Notification: Once the account is created, an email is automatically sent to the patient containing their login credentials and instructions for accessing their dashboard.
Patient Login: The patient logs into the platform to access their personalized dashboard, which will store all meeting summaries and provide communication tools for follow-up questions.

Copy and Paste: The patient or doctor copies the meeting link (G meet), pastes it into the platform, and clicks "Submit."
AI Bot Joins: The intelligent bot automatically joins the meeting, waits until it ends, and captures essential details such as treatment plans, medication schedules, and follow-up appointments.
Multilingual Support: During this process, the bot processes content for both English and the patient’s native language to ensure comprehensive understanding.

Email Summary: Within 2–3 minutes after the meeting concludes, the bot generates and sends a detailed summary to the patient via email.
Platform Update: The same summary is securely updated on the patient’s dashboard, where they can review it at any time.
Doctor-Patient Communication: If the patient has any questions or requires clarification, they can use the platform's messaging system to directly reach out to the doctor.
